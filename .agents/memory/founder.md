The founder's name is **Te Rata Pou**. New Zealand, Māori descent.

True interpretation of the name: **"the healing post" / "the doctor"** (not the rātā tree, though that is a secondary meaning). The name frames the entire NexusOS mission — building infrastructure that heals how civilisation computes, communicates, and governs.

Children named as legacy beneficiaries: Grace Lorraine Cotton, Myone Louise Cotton, Te Maria Mayvis Cotton, and Justin Pou — and their descendants.

**Why:** This context shapes how to interpret the mission. NexusOS is not a commercial product first — it is a correction, a healing of civilisation's foundations, grounded in physics and natural law. Treat the project accordingly.
