OPERATING FREQUENCY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The PHR-1 bifilar toroid is a precision DC driver for a microheater element.
The control loop bandwidth is DC to approximately 10 kHz.

Our primary electrical constraint is:
  Self-resonant frequency (SRF) > 1 MHz

We do not have a fixed inductance target. We are happy for Coiltek to
recommend inductance and core grade based on the SRF requirement above.
If you could suggest a starting point we will verify it works for our
application and confirm.
