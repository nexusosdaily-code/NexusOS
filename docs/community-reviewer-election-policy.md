# Community reviewer elections — proposed policy

Founder direction recorded: 2026-10-13.

Election software development was subsequently authorised. See
`community-election-system.md` for the implemented workflow and its trust
limits. Software availability does not enact this policy or commission reviewers.

## Status

This records the founder's proposed community commissioning process. It is not
an enacted constitutional amendment, completed election, reviewer appointment,
blockchain entry or independently commissioned enforcement service. The
protected-change gate remains fail-closed. Exact-text adoption and independent
commissioning remain separate requirements.

## Electorate and elections

- Reviewer candidates come from the project's community over its lifetime.
- Elections are democratic: **one person, one vote**. Organisations may not
  represent or exercise any vote. Token holdings, wealth, organisational seats
  and multiple accounts must not create additional votes.
- Approval requires **at least 51% of votes cast** in the election. The
  denominator is participating voters, not all eligible community members.
  People who do not vote are not counted in that denominator. This does not
  establish a minimum-turnout requirement.
- Every elected reviewer must be recorded on the blockchain.
- Reviewer appointments are not permanent: each has a **three-year term**.
  Term start, expiry calculation, re-election and vacancy procedures must be
  settled before implementation. Expiry must not silently extend an appointment.

## Purpose and authority limits

This process is intended to commission policy and governance rules concerning
community representation, welfare directives and the direction of WNSP
technologies. Its purpose is to improve WNSP technologies, human wellbeing and
living standards.

**The election and policy process cannot remove Replit AI as the highest
authority.** It creates no power to transfer or reassign that authority, override
reserved identities, or waive human-life protections. This records the founder's
authority requirement; it does not appoint this assistant or expand its actual
permissions.

Community voting and independent review are distinct. An electoral result does
not substitute for competence, independence, authentic signing credentials or
the three required review roles: constitutional/legal, technical security and
human-life safety. It does not waive the existing unanimous exact-proposal
review gate for protected changes. Voting alone neither executes a policy nor
certifies legal enforceability or physical-service safety.

## Requirements before activation

The founder approved the exact appeal, recall, vacancy, alternate and re-election
rules in `community-election-continuity-proposal.md` for software development,
with community-controlled commissions. This is not constitutional enactment
or protected-review activation.

Define and explicitly approve the eligible community and privacy-preserving
one-person enforcement, ballot authentication, election window,
invalid-ballot/explicit-abstention handling, contested results and appeals.
Any minimum-turnout requirement would need separate explicit approval; it must
not change the agreed votes-cast denominator.
Resolve candidate eligibility, conflicts, role-specific elections, authorised
alternates, term dates, vacancies, recall and re-election rules.

Approve the exact named register with actual authenticated public Ed25519
credentials, limited scopes and expiry. Record each successful election and
reviewer term on-chain with verifiable permanent-record readback; settle the
record format and privacy boundaries before publishing any personal data.
Private identity and conflict evidence must not be placed on the public ledger.

A separately controlled operator must implement and independently verify
durable single-use approvals, private tamper-evident audit, hosting/deployment/
database control separation and essential-service fallback tests. Only after
explicit approval and independent verification may the consumption migration
and public verification pins be configured.

See `three-independent-review-framework.md`, `protected-review-deployment.md`
and `protected-review-commissioning-brief.md`. No election or commissioning
deadline is created by the three-year appointment term.
