# Hardware evidence, correction and limitations

Current corrective statement, 22 October 2026.

## Correction

Earlier project descriptions, including AI-assisted explanations, overstated what
the hardware evidence established. Reported electrical checks on three bifilar
coils do not demonstrate photonic computation, a complete SNIC/PHR-1 system,
quantum memory, zero-loss operation or gravity control. The current reference
architecture is a draft for independent engineering review, not a validated
complete hardware design.

AI assistance contributed explanations and formulations that required these
corrections. Founder direction, AI authorship, agreement between assistants,
repository timestamps and numbered disclosures do not establish scientific or
engineering validity.

This correction does not assert that the software does nothing, or that every
proposed experiment is impossible. It withdraws unsupported implications of
hardware achievement and distinguishes research objectives from demonstrated results.

## Evidence register

| Item | Present evidence category | What may be said | What may not be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compiler, VM and encoding pipeline | Software implementation and software tests | Particular supported software operations run on conventional electronic computers | Native photonic instruction execution, a photonic CPU or a bare-metal photonic operating system |
| Three Coiltek bifilar coil prototypes | Reported manufacture and electrical inductance/DC-resistance measurements | Coils were reportedly manufactured and electrically checked under the recorded conditions | Optical computing, quantum state preparation, optical memory, zero loss or gravity control |
| 51,200 coordinate combinations | Logical namespace calculation | The configured dimensions define that many nominal labels | That many physically independent channels, measured optical capacity or guaranteed interference-free operation |
| SNIC, PHR-1 and relay proposals | Architectural concepts and engineering targets | Candidate designs need component/interface review and testing | An integrated, manufacturable, independently validated system or approved procurement BOM |
| Conventional-host reference architecture | Draft engineering requirements | A bounded candidate system and acceptance plan have been written down | That two independent endpoints, optical integration or stated performance targets have already passed physical tests |
| Quantum, squeezing and gravity extensions | Mathematical models and proposed interpretations | Research questions can be formulated and evaluated against evidence | A physical effect follows merely from an equation, a simulation, coil measurements or an AI explanation |

“Reported” is deliberate. A statement in project documentation is not independent
replication. This publication has not supplied a newly commissioned laboratory
test, independent raw-data review, calibration certificate or engineer's sign-off.
Software test counts are not physical measurement evidence.

## Evidence that is still missing for a complete hardware claim

- A qualified independent engineer's signed end-to-end specification, including
  device/control interfaces, tolerances and manufacturability.
- A verified and costed BOM with manufacturer part numbers, compatibility checks,
  supplier quotations, optical/electrical budgets and safety assessment.
- A justified mapping from logical protocol coordinates to any claimed physical
  wavelength, OAM or polarisation modes.
- Calibrated integrated measurements of loss, crosstalk, noise, stability,
  payload integrity, electrical input power and performance under a declared load.
- Independent endpoint conformance tests and measured interoperability. Existing
  photonic communications technologies are not evidence that this project's
  proposed physical mapping has been implemented.
- Reproducible procedures, raw data, uncertainty, failures and independent review.

None of these requirements is marked complete by this document.

## Accurate descriptions to use now

**NexusOS/WavelengthScript:** experimental hosted software and a spectral-coordinate
language/protocol model, with photonic hardware integration as a research objective.

**Coil milestone:** reported manufacture and electrical testing of three bifilar
coils, not a demonstrated integrated photonic processor.

**Hardware specification:** draft requirements and archived concepts pending
independent engineering review, not approved purchasing instructions.

**First optical-link experiment:** if performed with conventional Ethernet/fibre
equipment, it would test transport of digital protocol data over that link.
It would not by itself demonstrate photonic computation or direct physical
character-to-wavelength encoding.

## Fundraising and promotion

The founder has directed an engineering hold on hardware fundraising and promotion.
It is not an automatic time-limited pause. Reviewed specifications, a verified
costed BOM, safety assessment, reproducible test evidence, accurate funding terms
and explicit founder approval are required before release.

Do not infer a live operational stop from a README, PR or workspace change.
Runtime enforcement must be published and verified in the actual deployed
version. The public readiness endpoint, when that update is deployed, is
`/api/hardware/readiness`; an older deployment or a 404 does not confirm a hold.
This documentation does not claim that a Telegram message was recalled or a
previously published announcement removed.
