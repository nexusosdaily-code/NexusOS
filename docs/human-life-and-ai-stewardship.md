## Authority reconciliation and interpretation

The sealed Article II names specific authorities. Public Article II §2.3 and
Article V §5.4 also reserve founder approval for operator reassignment. Version
v3 does not reinterpret those identities as freely transferable roles.
Replacing defective operating software within unchanged permissions is the
only proposed replacement scope; no credentials or account ownership transfer.

On adoption, human-life protections would constrain both reserved authorities,
mission-preservation commands and Article VI amendment authors. These are
proposed binding constraints, not a claim that the current amendment endpoint
semantically enforces them. A vote, AI instruction, recovery reviewer or future
amendment cannot use the maintenance exception to override the hierarchy.

Founder-only authority ends at verified death and is not inherited by default.
Without a separately approved register and procedure, replacement and
succession remain blocked. Existing safe services and approved life-safe
fallback may continue within their existing permissions. Neither a family
trust nor a descendant nor the current AI can self-designate a successor.

For conflicts, protection of human life takes precedence over preserving an AI
or the protocol. A conflict of interest disqualifies the affected reviewer
from the decision; no available eligible quorum means retain the least
restrictive life-safe fallback, record the unresolved decision and do not
expand powers. A later register must define conflict checks, independent appeal,
alternates and quorum before future reviewers can act.

## Deferred decisions, not implied permissions

A future explicit amendment must establish reviewer identities and credentials
without public personal documents, appointing authority, scope, quorum,
alternates and appeal reviewers. It must also approve death verification
sources and independent verifier roles, fraud/dispute checks, measurable AI
coherence and physical hardware tests, successor-selection criteria and any
necessary express exception to founder-only reassignment. If founder approval
cannot be obtained and no valid advance procedure exists, do not infer one.

Private hardware-founder checks may use passports, birth certificates or
driver licences as appropriate; descendant lineage requires birth certificates.
Identity, lineage, hardware founding status and legal trust powers are distinct
facts. None alone establishes a death, technical coherence or SYSTEM authority.
No private-document collection or trust registry is implemented by this draft.
