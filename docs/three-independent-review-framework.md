# Three independent reviews for protected NexusOS/WNSP decisions

Date: 2026-10-12

## Status

The founder has since clarified that the community developed over the project's
lifetime will establish the real reviewer bodies and independent enforcement.
The selected reviewer candidates will come from that community over its lifetime.
Appointment and commissioning are deferred to that process, not activated by
this document. The founder's subsequent proposed democratic election rules are
recorded in `community-reviewer-election-policy.md`: one person, one vote, no
organisational votes, approval by at least 51% of votes cast,
on-chain elected-reviewer records and three-year appointments. That process
cannot remove Replit AI as highest authority. No election, enactment or approval
bypass is created by these documents.

The founder requires all three independent reviews for constitutional override
or removal/transfer of SYSTEM authority and has delegated selection of the
recommended review structure. This document specifies that structure for
review. It does not appoint reviewers, adopt an amendment, transfer authority,
commission a safety service or certify legal enforceability.

The previously reviewed stewardship text remains a historical exact-text
approval, not approval of the new wording below. It must not be presented as
already implementing this three-review requirement.

## Recommended review bodies

1. **Constitutional and legal review.** A qualified, independently controlled
   legal/governance reviewer checks the permitted scope, authority hierarchy,
   participant obligations, governing law, due process, and any trust or
   succession instrument. Global participation does not eliminate applicable
   national law. The governing jurisdiction remains undecided.
2. **Technical security review.** A separately controlled security assessor
   checks identity, signatures, permissions, compromise evidence, proposed
   software or authority changes, bypass resistance and tested recovery.
   The assessor must not be the implementer or proposed recipient of authority.
3. **Human-life safety review.** A separately controlled safety/ethics assessor
   checks the concrete harm pathway, rights, essential-service continuity,
   proportionality, alternatives and relevant domain-specific safety tests.
   A general software review is not certification of physical life-critical
   equipment.

These are roles, not appointed organisations. Each body must have a named,
authenticated accountable signatory and demonstrable expertise. AI may assist
analysis but cannot constitute all three independent approvals or approve its
own authority change. No particular professional or organisation has been
selected, contacted or certified as independent.

## Independence and approval rules

- Three signatories must have separate controlling interests and signing
  identities. Three accounts, departments or models controlled by one actor
  are not three independent reviews.
- Record funding, ownership, conflicts, competence and recusal requirements.
  Hiring a reviewer does not prove independence. Material conflicts require
  recusal and an already authorised alternate, not a convenient substitute.
- Each review approves the exact same proposal: its text, action, affected
  identities, permissions, evidence, execution artefacts and recovery plan.
  Each review records reasons, contrary evidence, uncertainty and conditions.
- Bind authenticated approvals to a proposal digest, approved register
  version, scope and expiry. Changed proposals need fresh approvals. Prevent
  replay, forged approvals, expired approvals and combining approvals for
  different proposals.
- Require **3 of 3 approvals**. Refusal, recusal without an authorised
  alternate, unavailable reviewer, incomplete evidence or an unresolved
  condition blocks the protected action. No founder, AI, vote or administrator
  can count as all three or bypass this requirement.
- Approval is a prerequisite, not unrestricted power. Enforce existing
  succession eligibility and reserved-identity constraints as additional
  gates. Reviewers cannot mint authority or appoint themselves.
- Human-life safeguards remain non-waivable, including by unanimous approval.
  Review validates a permitted change; it cannot legitimise wrongful harm,
  unlawful conduct or sacrificing people to preserve NexusOS.

## Decision boundaries

| Decision | Recommended boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Constitutional amendment or claimed constitutional override | Exact-text review by all three, lawful and constitutionally permitted scope, and explicit authenticated append-only adoption. No waiver of human-life safeguards. |
| Removal, reassignment or transfer of SYSTEM authority; succession appointment | All three reviews plus separate express constitutional authority, verified eligibility and commissioned enforcement. Currently disabled. |
| Authority-bearing AI replacement or a change to safety/authority boundaries | All three reviews and verified safety/security tests; calling it “maintenance” cannot bypass the gate. |
| Read-only inspection or routine maintenance within unchanged authority and safety boundaries | Existing bounded maintenance rules; escalation to protected review whenever scope or safety significance is uncertain. |
| Immediate containment of a credible dangerous capability | Only an explicitly pre-approved and tested mechanism, proportionate restriction, protected evidence and immediate notification. No emergency authority transfer or constitutional change. Preserve life-safe essential services. |

Retain the existing proposed 24-hour founder review of urgent containment while
the founder is living. It is not permission to remove or transfer SYSTEM
authority. A missed deadline never automatically restores a dangerous
capability. The qualified safety reviewer must verify service-specific fallback
plans before physical deployment; none is commissioned by this document.

## Proposed constitutional addendum

This is new wording for exact-text review, not an enacted rule:

<!-- three-review-addendum:start -->
NexusOS/WNSP constitutional human-life protections bind every participating authority and operator regardless of operating country, subject to applicable law. No person may be sacrificed to preserve the protocol, AI, assets or mission; no vote or unanimous review may waive these protections.

No unilateral human or AI instruction, governance vote or emergency claim may override the constitutions or remove, reassign or transfer SYSTEM authority. Any constitutionally permitted protected change requires prior documented approval of the same exact proposal by all three separately controlled, authenticated review bodies: constitutional/legal, technical security, and human-life safety.

The approved register must define signatories, competence, independence, conflicts, alternates, narrow powers, appeal, signature scope and expiry. An unavailable or disqualified reviewer, refusal or unresolved condition blocks the change. Reviewers cannot appoint themselves, acquire unrestricted SYSTEM control or bypass reserved-identity and succession conditions.

Authority-bearing replacement and changes to safety or authority boundaries require the same unanimous review; a maintenance label is not an exception. Approved urgent containment may restrict a dangerous capability while preserving life-safe essential services, but cannot transfer authority or change the constitution. Preserve evidence, notify immediately, and obtain the required review; missed review does not restore danger automatically.

Approval alone does not execute a change. Adoption is authenticated and append-only, preserving the original seal. Execution additionally requires independently commissioned enforcement, applicable legal instruments and verified safety conditions. No register, authority transfer, succession appointment or worldwide legal enforceability is established merely by this text.
<!-- three-review-addendum:end -->

## Sensible implementation order

1. Review the exact revised wording and reconcile it with the earlier
   founder-only maintenance provisions. Keep wording approval distinct from
   consent to submit an amendment.
2. Select a home legal structure with qualified counsel; prepare global
   participation obligations and required local instruments. Do not assume
   that outsiders, countries or courts are bound by the charter.
3. Approve named review bodies and alternates, independence evidence and their
   limited appointment/appeal procedure. No live reviewer register exists
   merely because three roles have been recommended.
4. Implement and test a dedicated unanimous protected-change gate outside the
   affected operator's sole control. An existing two-of-three safety-review
   mode is not this gate and does not authorise authority transfer.
5. Formally adopt the exact approved amendment through the authenticated
   process. An amendment receipt is not commissioning or legal certification.
6. Commission the external enforcement boundary and service-specific recovery
   plans; verify genuine control separation and remove operator bypass.

Test at minimum: only two approvals, a shared controller, a recused reviewer,
an expired approval, altered wording or artefacts, replay, self-appointment,
founder/AI bypass, disagreement, reviewer outage, an emergency transfer attempt,
and loss of essential-service continuity. All protected changes must remain
blocked until the required register, approvals and execution gates are valid.
